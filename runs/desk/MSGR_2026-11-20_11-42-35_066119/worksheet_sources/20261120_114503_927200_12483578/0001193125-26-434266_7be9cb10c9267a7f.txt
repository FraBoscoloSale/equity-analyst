Item 1.01
Entry into a Material Definitive Agreement.
Completion of Distribution
On October 26, 2026, MSG Knickerbockers Corp. (formerly Madison Square Garden Sports Corp. and referred herein as “MSG Knicks”) effected the distribution (the “Distribution”) of all of the issued and outstanding shares of the common stock of MSG Rangers Corp. (formerly MSGS Spinco, Inc., and referred to herein as the “Company”). In the Distribution, (a) each holder of MSG Knicks Class A common stock, par value $0.01 per share (“MSG Knicks Class A Common Stock”), received one share of the Company’s Class A common stock, par value $0.01 per share (“Company Class A Common Stock”), for every two shares of MSG Knicks Class A Common Stock held of record as of the close of business, New York City time, on October 20, 2026 (the “Record Date”) and (b) each holder of MSG Knicks Class B common stock, par value $0.01 per share (“MSG Knicks Class B Common Stock”), received one share of the Company’s Class B common stock, par value $0.01 per share (“Company Class B Common Stock”) for every two shares of MSG Knicks Class B Common Stock held of record as of the close of business, New York City time, on the Record Date. In the Distribution, an aggregate of 9,794,568 shares of Company Class A Common Stock and 2,264,754 shares of Company Class B Common Stock were distributed, with any fractional shares converted to cash and paid to stockholders. In addition, in connection with the Distribution, 46,005 shares of Company Class A Common Stock were distributed in respect of MSG Knicks
non-employee
director restricted stock units to the applicable holders as of the Record Date.
NHL Transfer Consent Agreement
On October 23, 2026, MSG Knicks, the Company and certain of their subsidiaries entered into a Transfer Consent Agreement with the National Hockey League (“NHL”) pursuant to which the parties acknowledge that future transfers of the ownership of the New York Rangers hockey club (the “Rangers”), the location of the Rangers or certain other transfers of assets or ownership, subject to exceptions, will require the consent of the NHL. The Company parties and MSG Knicks parties also agreed to release the NHL entities from any claims against them.
The description above is qualified in its entirety by reference to the Transfer Consent Agreement, which is attached as Exhibits 10.1 to this Current Report on Form
8-K
and incorporated into this Item 1.01 by reference.
Item 5.02
Departure of Directors or Certain Officers; Election of Directors; Appointment of Certain Officers; Compensatory Arrangements of Certain Officers.
On October 26, 2026, Alan D. Schwartz and Hans Vestberg were appointed to serve as members of the Audit Committee of the Company’s Board of Directors (the “Board”), and Mr. Schwartz was appointed as Chair of the Audit Committee.
Also on October 26, 2026, Stephen C. Mills and Mr. Vestberg were appointed to serve as members of the Compensation Committee of the Board, and Joseph M. Cohen was appointed as Chair of the Compensation Committee.
Approval of Executive Deferred Compensation Plan
Effective as of October 26, 2026, the Board approved the MSG Rangers Corp. Executive Deferred Compensation Plan, (the “EDCP”), pursuant to which certain employees, including the Company’s named executive officers, may elect to participate.
Pursuant to the EDCP, participants may make elective base salary or bonus deferral contributions. Participants may make individual investment elections that will determine the rate of return on their deferral amounts under the EDCP. The EDCP does not provide any above-market returns or preferential earnings to participants, and the participants’ deferrals and their earnings are always 100% vested. The EDCP does not provide for any Company contributions. Participants may elect at the time they make their deferral elections to receive their distribution either as a lump sum payment or in substantially equal annual installments over a period of 5 years.
The description above is qualified in its entirety by reference to the EDCP, which is attached as Exhibit 10.2 hereto and incorporated into this Item 5.02 by reference.
